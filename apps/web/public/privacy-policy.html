Sled Privacy Policy

How information is handled across Sled sign-in, apps, and your personal Sled Connect infrastructure.

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 1, 2026

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes how Samoyed Labs (“we” or “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use the Sled desktop and mobile applications, the hosted web application at app.samoyedlabs.com, Sled Connect (together, the “Services”), or our public pages at app.samoyedlabs.com/about (the “Site”). It also covers information you provide to us directly.

Sled Connect provides shared sign-in while allowing you to deploy your own relay, database, and tunnels in your Cloudflare account. The distinctions below explain which information is handled by our shared authentication service and which is handled by infrastructure you control.

By using the Site or the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Site or the Services. This Policy does not govern third-party coding agents, artificial-intelligence providers, identity providers, or environments that you choose to connect to Sled; those third parties process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

02

Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information about you in different ways depending on how you use Sled and which optional features you enable.

Personal Information From Users of Sled

When you use Sled, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information. We use Clerk for account authentication and management. If you create or sign in to a Sled account, we receive identifiers and profile information such as your email address, name, profile image, user ID, sign-in method, and session or security records. Your account is stored in our Clerk instance.
  • Environment and device information. Your personal Sled Connect relay processes your Sled user ID, server identifiers and labels, public keys, hostnames, tunnel and DNS identifiers, connection configuration, credential hashes, and link timestamps. These records are stored in the D1 database in the Cloudflare account where you deploy the relay. The relay handles temporary credentials and authentication proofs to authorize access.
  • Automation webhooks. Webhook requests pass through your personal relay. If you enable holding requests while a server is offline, their bodies, headers, URL parameters, and delivery credentials may be stored temporarily in your D1 database. Background push notifications and remote Live Activity updates are not available through this version of Sled Connect.
  • Product usage data. The current host-server code inherits T3 Code analytics. Unless disabled, it sends product events to ingest.t3.codes, which forwards events to PostHog. Events include hashed account or installation identifiers, provider and model choices, feature usage, app and operating-system versions, turn results, durations, and available token totals. Hashed identifiers can link activity and are not anonymous. These events are designed to exclude prompts, responses, file contents, authentication tokens, and conversation identifiers. See “Product Usage Data” below to disable reporting.
  • Information you provide to us. We collect information that you choose to provide when you request support, send feedback, or otherwise communicate with us.

Google and Apple Sign-In

If you choose Google sign-in, Clerk receives your Google account identifier, email address and verification status, and basic profile information Google makes available, such as your name and profile image. We use this information to create or link your Sled account, show your profile, and authenticate and secure access. Google sign-in does not grant Sled Connect access to Gmail messages, Drive files, Calendar, or contacts. We do not use Google account data for advertising or AI model training.

If you choose Apple sign-in, Clerk receives your Apple account identifier and any name or email address Apple shares, which may be an Apple private relay email address. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

Personal Information From Users of Our Site Generally

When you visit the Site or use online portions of the Services, we and our service providers may automatically log online identifiers and internet-activity information, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, device and operating-system type, browser or app type and version, pages viewed, access times, request timestamps, connection events, latency, error details, and diagnostic traces. The amount of information we collect depends on the Services you use and your device and browser settings.

We use operational and diagnostic information to provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the Services, not for targeted advertising.

Information We Get From Others

We may receive personal information from service providers that help us operate the Services, such as authentication and identity providers, app stores, device-platform providers, and coding-agent providers you choose to use. We may combine this information with information we collect through the Site and the Services.

Cookies and Local Storage

The Site and online portions of the Services may use cookies or similar technologies to operate essential features, maintain sessions, remember preferences, and understand whether the Services are functioning correctly. Sled also stores connection settings, credentials, preferences, drafts, and cached thread, file, branch, and server metadata on your device. Credentials and session tokens use platform-provided protected storage where supported. Information stored only on your device is not collected by Samoyed Labs unless it is transmitted as part of a feature you use.

Your Own Infrastructure

The Cloudflare API token supplied during relay setup is stored as a secret in your Worker deployment. Signing into Sled does not give us administrative access to your Cloudflare account. Normal coding-session traffic, including prompts, responses, files, and terminal output, travels between your client and chosen server through its tunnel; the coordination relay does not store a copy of those sessions. Cloudflare and your chosen server and AI providers still process the information needed to provide those features. Cloudflare Tunnel is not a promise of end-to-end encryption that excludes Cloudflare.

Device Permissions

  • Camera. Used when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. Camera frames are processed on your device.
  • Photos and files. Items you select are accessed only to preview them or attach them to a request you initiate.
  • Local network. Used to discover or connect to Sled environments accessible from your machine over the network.

Your Choices Regarding Your Personal Information

Account and Connection Choices

You may use local or direct connections without signing in to a Sled account. You can disconnect environments, sign out, clear local caches, and change your own infrastructure settings. You can grant or revoke camera, photo, and local-network permissions in your device settings. Removing Sled access through Google or Apple does not itself delete your Sled account.

Product Usage Data

To stop sending product usage data, set the environment variable T3CODE_TELEMETRY_ENABLED=false where Sled runs, for example in your shell profile, and restart Sled.

Cookies and Local Data

You can use your browser settings to stop accepting or to delete cookies, although some portions of the Site or the Services may not function correctly as a result. Information stored only on your device remains there until you clear it, remove the relevant connection, or uninstall the app. The mobile app provides cache controls under Settings → App → Client Storage.

Retention and Deletion

We retain account, support, and security information while needed to provide the Services, protect against abuse, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Service-provider logs and backups follow the applicable provider and deployment retention settings.

To request deletion of your Sled account and information we control, email raajtarun951@gmail.com from your account email address. We may need to verify your identity.

You control information in your own relay, clients, and servers. Unlinking a server revokes its access and initiates tunnel cleanup, but the relay retains a revoked database record for cleanup and recovery. Deleting a Sled account does not automatically delete your personal Cloudflare database, local data, server data, or provider accounts. Delete those separately or contact their administrator.

Queued offline webhooks are deleted after completed delivery handling or during cleanup once they are more than 24 hours old. Cleanup normally runs every five minutes while the deployment is operating; stopped deployments and provider limits can delay it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of your personal information, or to restrict or object to certain processing. You may also have the right to appeal our response or complain to a data-protection authority. We will honor applicable rights after verifying your request.

Security of Your Personal Information

Samoyed Labs is committed to protecting the security of your personal information. We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These safeguards include encrypted network transport, scoped and proof-bound access tokens for supported connection flows, protected credential storage, and redaction of authorization headers from relay diagnostics.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we use reasonable efforts to protect personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. You are responsible for securing the environments, coding-agent accounts, credentials, and networks that you connect to Sled.
